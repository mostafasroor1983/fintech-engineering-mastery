## In one minute

Fifty scenario-based multiple-choice questions (1-50 of 100), mixed across payments, Java, Spring, distributed systems, databases, operations and interviewing. Each has one best answer and a short explanation that appears after you answer. **Budget: 30 minutes, closed book** (about 35 seconds per question). Your score appears at the end of the quiz.

## Rules for an honest score

1. No lessons open, no searching. The point is to find what you don't know.
2. Answer every question; if unsure, pick your best guess and note the number.
3. After scoring, read the explanation of every wrong **and every guessed** question.
4. Map each miss to its Q&A bank (payments: lesson 2, Java: 3, concurrency: 4, JVM: 5, Spring: 6-7, distributed: 8-9, databases: 10, architecture and operations: 11) and add it to your final-review list.

Target: **at least 43/50 (85%)**. Below 40, spend the first half of the final review block on the banks you missed most.

## After the test

- Write your score on your scoreboard.
- List your misses by bank; the bank with most misses gets a second pass in lesson 18.
- Re-take only the questions you missed after the final review, not the whole test.
