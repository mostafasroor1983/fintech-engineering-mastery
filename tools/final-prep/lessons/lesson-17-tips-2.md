## In one minute

150 short, actionable tips (151-300 of 300) on debugging live failures, behavioural and ownership answers, common interview traps, communication and staff-level signals, and the interview day itself. They're written to be skimmed: about 12 seconds each. **Budget: 30 minutes.** Don't try to memorise them all: copy the ten that hit your weak spots onto one page and re-read that page in the final review.

## How to use these tips

- Skim a section, and when a tip makes you think "I wouldn't have said that", copy it.
- Turn each copied tip into a one-sentence habit ("In every design, I say what happens on a timeout").
- Practise the habit once aloud in a mock answer today.
